Data, definitions, and how matching works

The snapshot is frozen at October 23, 2026; it is not a live financial feed. Monetary values are USD and fiscal dates are preserved. Missing data remains unavailable, not zero. Early IPO documents can disclose older periods later; inspect each disclosure date.

Matching uses annual revenue on a log scale, growth, operating margin, gross margin, sales costs, and R&D. Training-based scaling and clipping are fixed from the earlier 274-row sample. Candidate missing measures are skipped; at least three observed inputs and 80% weighted coverage are required. No future outcome or company identity enters the distance.

The company library includes the fuller annual history. Matching uses the strictly eligible historical sample and can be limited to early public-company years or training rows. A similar financial profile is not necessarily a similar product, industry, or management strategy. Adjusted regressions use observed complete cases and company-level bootstrap uncertainty. Strategy comparisons align a starting financial year, observed spending change, and explicit outcome period. These are historical associations, not causal effects or live news.

Observed year-to-year changes are stored only for adjacent annual periods with available inputs. Event definitions supply sourced calendar research windows; overlap does not establish company exposure. Source facts and field definitions are available through the API.
